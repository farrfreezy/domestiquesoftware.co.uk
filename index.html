Domestique Software Ltd builds and runs Routesmith, a web app for cyclists. This page says who we are, what we sell and how to reach us.

What we make

Routesmith picks the right route for the day. It scores the cycling routes you have already saved, from each one's own GPS track (the roads, the climbing, the stops), then reads the weather forecast for the day you are riding and tells you which one fits. Routes come in from the third-party services you already save them on. It runs in the browser and installs to a phone's home screen.

Plans and pricing

Every plan keeps your whole route library on your device. The free plan scores a few routes of your choice; Premium scores all of them. A new account gets Premium for a trial period, once per person.

Premium is a monthly subscription in pounds sterling, billed through Stripe on Stripe's own checkout and subscription pages. The current price, the billing period and the way to change or cancel are shown before you subscribe and on the subscription page afterwards.

Cancellation and refunds

  • Cancel at any time from the subscription page. Premium runs to the end of the period already paid for and is not refunded for the remainder, except where the law gives you a right to a refund.
  • If you subscribe as a consumer you have fourteen days from subscribing to change your mind. Premium starts straight away, so cancelling inside those days is refunded less the days already used.
  • If a payment fails, Premium continues while the payment is retried and ends when the subscription does. Nothing is deleted when a plan changes, only the scores beyond what the plan covers.
  • Deleting your account cancels any subscription at once.

The full wording is in the Routesmith terms of use.
